# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for analyzing e-commerce sales performance. It provides key business insights by tracking revenue, orders, customer ratings, product categories, and regional performance through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

## Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Analyze revenue across regions and product categories.
* Track order volume and customer ratings.
* Identify sales trends over time.
* Support data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.

## Dashboard KPIs

* **Total Revenue:** 5.11M
* **Total Orders:** 63M
* **Average Customer Rating:** 2.97
* **Average Revenue per Order:** 1.02K

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes:

* Revenue trend by year
* Revenue by region
* Revenue by product category
* Interactive filters for region and order date
* Business performance KPIs

## Key Insights

* Electronics generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
* Revenue is distributed across four main regions (West, North, South, and East).
* Interactive slicers enable users to filter results by region and order date.
* KPIs provide a quick overview of business performance.
